KAS Exam Pattern

Prelims2 objective papers - 200 marks each
MainsQualifying language papers + Essay + 4 GS papers
Personality TestInterview stage after written examination

The preliminary examination is objective. The Main Examination is descriptive and includes qualifying Kannada and English papers, an Essay paper and four General Studies papers.

Preliminary Examination

Paper I - 200 marks, 100 questions, 2 hours

General Studies related to National & International importance

  • Current events of national and international importance.

Humanities

  • History of India - broad social, economic, cultural and political understanding, with focus on the Indian National Movement and special emphasis on Karnataka.
  • World Geography and Geography of India, with focus on Karnataka.
  • Indian Polity and Economy - political system, rural development, planning and economic reforms, sustainable development, poverty alleviation, demographics and social-sector initiatives.

Paper II - 200 marks, 100 questions, 2 hours

State Importance

  • Current events of Karnataka importance.
  • Important Karnataka Government programmes.

General Science, Technology, Environment & Ecology

  • Contemporary developments in science and technology and their implications.
  • Matters of everyday observation and experience expected from a well-educated person without specialised scientific study.
  • General issues on health, environmental ecology, biodiversity and climate change.

General Mental Ability

  • Comprehension.
  • Logical reasoning and analytical ability.
  • Decision-making and problem-solving.
  • Basic numeracy and data interpretation at SSLC / Class X level.
Negative markingThe Gazetted Probationers notification provides for a penalty of one-fourth of the marks assigned to a question for a wrong answer.

Main Examination

Kannada150 marks - qualifying - 2 hours
English150 marks - qualifying - 2 hours
Essay250 marks - 3 hours
GS I250 marks - 3 hours
GS II250 marks - 3 hours
GS III250 marks - 3 hours
GS IV250 marks - 3 hours

Kannada and English are qualifying papers. The 2023-24 Gazetted Probationers notification prescribes a minimum of 35% in each qualifying paper. The merit-bearing written papers total 1250 marks.

Essay - 250 marks

  • Essay on a topic of national / international importance.
  • Essay on a topic of state / local importance.

General Studies I

History & Cultural Heritage - India and Karnataka

  • Cultural heritage of India and Karnataka.
  • Modern History of India, including the freedom movement.
  • Karnataka history - major dynasties and cultural developments, including Kadambas, Chalukyas, Rashtrakutas, Hoysalas and Vijayanagara.
  • Modern Mysore, Karnataka freedom movement and unification of Karnataka.

Social & Political Perspective

  • Post-independence Karnataka.
  • Social change and modernisation.
  • Political system and government.
  • Decentralisation of power.
  • Socio-economic system, rural society and development.

Indian Economy, Planning & Rural Development

  • Indian Economy and development patterns.
  • Planning and planning goals.
  • Decentralisation and rural-development initiatives.
  • India and international economic relations.
  • Data collection, analysis and interpretation.

General Studies II

Physical Features & Natural Resources

  • Lithosphere, atmosphere and hydrosphere.
  • Continental physiography and demographic distribution.
  • Indian physiography and natural resources.
  • Physiographic divisions of Karnataka, industrial planning, urbanisation and land-use issues.

Indian Constitution & Polity

  • Nature and overview of the Indian Constitution.
  • Fundamental Rights and related constitutional provisions.
  • Distribution of legislative powers.
  • Union and State legislatures, important constitutional amendments and welfare machinery.
  • Union and State services.

Public Administration, Management & International Relations

  • Public and private administration; organisation structure.
  • Organisational behaviour and management concepts, techniques and tools.
  • Administrative reforms.
  • United Nations and specialised agencies; international relations relevant to India and Karnataka.

General Studies III

Science & Technology

  • Role and impact of science and technology in Indian development.
  • Information technology in the public domain.
  • Basics of science and technology, space, energy resources and emerging technologies.
  • Science and technology for rural upliftment and knowledge society.

Life Sciences, Agriculture & Health

  • Advances in natural sciences and life sciences.
  • Agriculture, sericulture, horticulture and biotechnology initiatives.
  • Animal husbandry, dairy, fisheries and veterinary sciences.
  • Agricultural development policies, programmes and trade.
  • Hygiene and health.

Environment & Ecology

  • Environment conservation and natural resources.
  • Biodiversity and ecosystems.
  • Solid-waste management and pollution.
  • Environmental challenges in development.
  • Role of information technology in environment and human health; state-community-civil-society interface.

General Studies IV - Ethics, Integrity & Aptitude

  • Ethics - determinants, essence and consequences.
  • Ethics in public administration.
  • Accountability and ethical governance.
  • Right to Information and concept of public service.
  • Human values.
  • Integrity, impartiality, objectivity and non-partisanship.
  • Aptitude and foundational values for civil services.
  • Emotional intelligence.
  • Contributions of social and moral thinkers.
  • Case studies based on ethical and administrative issues.

Personality Test

Qualified candidates are called for a personality test. The 2023-24 notification listed the Personality Test at 25 marks, with written merit papers carrying 1250 marks.
